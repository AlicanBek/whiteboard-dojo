## 💡 Tips for Using These Challenges

- **Beginner challenges** are great for getting started and building confidence with foundational skills
- **Intermediate challenges** test your ability to think through complex scenarios, special user needs, and system interactions
- **Advanced challenges** push you to consider multiple stakeholders, emerging technologies, ethical considerations, and complex business constraints

Choose challenges based on your skill level or use a mix to keep your practice sessions fresh and engaging!
