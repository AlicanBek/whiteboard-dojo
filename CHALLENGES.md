## 💡 Tips for Using These Challenges

- **Beginner challenges** are great for getting started and building confidence
- **Intermediate challenges** test your ability to think through complex scenarios and user needs
- **Advanced challenges** push you to consider multiple stakeholders, accessibility, and business constraints

Choose challenges based on your skill level or use a mix to keep your practice sessions fresh!
